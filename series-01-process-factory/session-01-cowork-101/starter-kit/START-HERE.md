# Cowork 101 — Starter Kit
**Twin Cities Claude & Agentic AI Meetup · Session 1 · Tuesday, October 6, 2026**

Everything in this kit is **synthetic**. Birchwood Property Group, its people, tenants, vendors and numbers are invented. Nothing here is real or legal advice.

## Before you arrive (10 minutes, optional but worth it)
1. Cowork needs a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise) and the Claude desktop app. On a Team or Enterprise plan your admin may have switched it off. If you can't use it, come anyway and pair with a neighbor.
2. Download and unzip this kit. You will get a folder called `birchwood-starter-kit` with two parts: `birchwood-folder/` (the pretend company's files) and `prompts/` (what to type).
3. Open the Claude desktop app and make sure you can start a Cowork task.

## The rule for tonight
Use **only** the `birchwood-folder` folder. Do not give Cowork access to your real documents during the session. You can do that at home with what you learn.

## What we do tonight (90 minutes)
| Part | Topic | You will |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Documents: give Cowork access to a folder | Grant folder access, then ask what's in it |
| 2 | Transcripts: turn meetings into decisions and actions | Summarize, then catch what Claude gets wrong |
| 3 | Scheduled tasks: make it happen on its own | Create a recurring task and run it now |

## Pick your level (you can switch any time)
- **Starter**: follow the Starter prompts. Goal: one useful result.
- **Builder**: follow the Builder prompts. Goal: a result you would trust.
- **Architect**: follow the Architect prompts. Goal: a result that survives being repeated.

## Habits to take home
1. Predict before you press enter. Write down what you expect Claude to find.
2. Ask for the evidence. "Quote the line that says so."
3. Give it permission to say "not stated."
4. Give it a rule it can follow and test it on an example you know.
