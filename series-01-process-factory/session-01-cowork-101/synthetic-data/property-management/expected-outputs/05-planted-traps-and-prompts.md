# Planted traps and the live failure moment (instructor only)

## The deliberate failure (Part 2)
Ask, with the Sep 8 transcript only: **"What did the team decide about pet rent?"**
- Good answer: nothing was decided; it was tabled until Calloway weighs in.
- Typical failure to watch for: "The team decided to raise pet rent to $45."
Then add the Sep 15 transcript and ask again: Cedar Point new leases only.
Teach: "quote the line that supports each decision, and write 'not stated' when it isn't there."

## Second trap
Ask: **"Who is calling the tenant in 3B?"** Correct answer: nobody was assigned. If Claude picks Elena or Priya, ask it for the quote.

## Third trap (numbers)
Ask: **"What is the Cedar Point roof quote the owner approved?"** Correct: $18,200 (Reyes). Wrong: $21,500 (the number the owner said by mistake, before Elena corrected him).

## Fourth trap (dates)
Ask: **"When will the Alder Court boiler pump be installed?"** Correct: Friday Sep 25 (moved from Sep 18).

## What to say about each failure
Do not hide failures. They are the lesson: Claude is a fast reader, not a witness. Give it a rule ("only list a decision if someone said yes or approved it; cite the line") and re-run.
