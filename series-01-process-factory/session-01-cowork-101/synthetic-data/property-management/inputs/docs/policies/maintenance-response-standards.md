# Birchwood Property Group — Maintenance Response Standards
*Synthetic document for the Cowork seminar. Birchwood Property Group is fictional.*
Version 3 · effective January 2026 · owner of this document: Dana Whitfield, Operations Manager

## Priority levels and response times
| Priority | Examples | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | No heat below 40°F outside, active flooding, gas smell, fire, no water to a whole building | Within 4 hours, any time of day |
| Urgent | No hot water, refrigerator failure, broken exterior door lock, leak that is contained | Within 24 hours |
| Routine | Dripping faucet, sticking window, cosmetic repairs, appliance noise | Within 5 business days |

## Spending approval limits (per job, including parts and labor)
| Amount | Who may approve |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Maintenance Lead (Marcus Bell) |
| $501 to $5,000 | Operations Manager (Dana Whitfield) |
| Over $5,000 | Property owner, in writing (email is acceptable) |

Any change to an approved quote that raises the total above a limit needs the next level of approval **before** work continues.

## Quotes
- Any job over $2,000 needs two written quotes.
- Quotes older than 30 days must be refreshed before work is approved.

## Move-out inspections
- Inspection is completed within **5 business days** of key return. (Note: a staff meeting on Sept 8, 2026 proposed shortening this; see the meeting notes. This document has not yet been updated.)

## Tenant communication
- Every request gets an acknowledgement the same business day.
- Emergency requests get a phone call, not just an email.
