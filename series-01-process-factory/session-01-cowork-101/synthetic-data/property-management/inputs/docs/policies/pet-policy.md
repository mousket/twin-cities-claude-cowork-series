*Synthetic document. Not legal advice. Birchwood is fictional.*

- Maximum two pets per unit. Cats and dogs under 60 lb only. Assistance animals are handled under a separate process and are not subject to pet rent or pet deposits.
- **Pet rent: $35 per pet per month.**

- Pets must be registered with the office within 7 days of move-in or lease signing.
