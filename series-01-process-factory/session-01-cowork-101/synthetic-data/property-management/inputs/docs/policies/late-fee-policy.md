# Birchwood Property Group — Rent Due Dates and Late Fees
*Synthetic document. Not legal advice. Check local law before using any real late-fee policy.*
Version 4 · effective July 2025

- Rent is due on the 1st of each month.
- Grace period: through the 3rd of the month.
- Late fee: flat $50 on the 4th, plus $5 per day after, capped at $100 total per month.
- Payment plans must be requested in writing before the 10th and approved by the Operations Manager.
- Delinquency report: the bookkeeper sends the previous month's report to the Operations Manager by the 10th.
- Notices to tenants are sent only by the Operations Manager or the property manager of that building.
