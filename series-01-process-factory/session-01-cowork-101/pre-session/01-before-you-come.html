1The short version

Five things
  1. Have a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise) and the Claude desktop app.
  2. Download the starter kit (page 2: Get the starter kit).
  3. Do the 3-minute practice test in an empty folder, so you know folder access works on your machine.

No paid plan? No laptop? Come anyway. Sit with a neighbor, watch, and use the recording and kit afterward. Nobody gets left behind, and pairing is a normal way to work in this room.

4The 3-minute practice test

This proves three things at once: your plan works, Cowork opens, and Cowork can work in a folder you choose. It uses a throwaway folder, not the starter kit and not any real files.

  1. Make an empty folder named cowork-practice somewhere simple, such as Documents.
  2. Open the Claude desktop app, and in the message box select Cowork.
  3. Choose cowork-practice as the folder to work in. The exact control for choosing a folder may be labeled differently in your version, and your operating system may ask for permission. We'll show it live Tuesday. If you can't find it, that's useful: write it down and ask us.
  4. Paste this request: 
    Create a text file named hello.txt in this folder. It should say "Cowork works" and today's date. Then tell me the file name.
  5. Look in the folder. You should see hello.txt. Cowork may ask you to approve the action first; approving is fine for this practice folder only.
Success

You see hello.txt in the practice folder. You're ready.

Not working?

See If you're stuck. Don't spend more than 15 minutes on it. Come anyway: an early arrival with a helper usually fixes it faster than an evening alone.

More detail on installing

  • Go to claude.ai/download and pick your system. Open the downloaded file to finish installing.
  • Launch Claude (Applications on Mac, Start menu on Windows) and sign in with your account.
  • Updates arrive inside the app on Mac and Windows.

5Privacy and good habits

Do
  • Use only the birchwood-folder from the kit.
  • Treat the kit as disposable: re-download it any time.
  • Predict before you press enter. Write down what you think Claude will find.
  • Ask Claude to quote the line that supports an answer.
Don't
  • Connect Documents, Desktop or Downloads as a whole.
  • Put real client, tenant or employee data in the session.
  • Work around your employer's IT or admin settings.
  • Assume an answer is right because it sounds confident.
Why the folder rule matters

The folder you connect is the folder Claude can read and quote. Everything in our kit is made up: Birchwood Property Group, its tenants, vendors and numbers are fictional. That's deliberate, so you can see Cowork work without risking anything real.

7If you're stuck

I don't see “Cowork” in the app
  • Update the Claude desktop app and restart it.
  • Confirm you're on a paid plan and signed in to the right account.
  • On Team or Enterprise, an admin controls availability. Ask them. Don't try to bypass it.
  • Use the desktop app. Anthropic's documentation says tasks that use files on your computer need the desktop app open so Claude can reach them.
I can't choose a folder, or my computer asks for permission
  • Your operating system may ask whether Claude can access a folder. Allow it for your practice folder or the starter kit only.
  • Put the folder somewhere simple, like Documents, and try again.
  • On a managed laptop, IT policy may block it. Bring the question Tuesday and pair up.
Claude says I've reached a usage limit
  • Cowork tasks use more of your allowance than chatting. Wait for your allowance to reset, or pair up for the session.
  • Avoid heavy Cowork use on Tuesday before the session.
I can't download the kit at work
  • Some company networks block Google Drive or GitHub. Try your phone's hotspot, or download it at home.
  • If nothing works, tell a helper at the door. We'll get a copy to you, which is one more reason to arrive early.
The practice test worked but I'm nervous about the real session

That's normal. The kit is fictional, you can't break anything important, and there are helpers in the room. If something goes wrong, we get to see something real about how these tools behave, and that's part of the lesson.

8Glossary

CoworkThe mode of Claude's desktop app where Claude works on tasks for you, including in folders you choose.
Folder accessLetting Cowork see files in a folder. Only share folders you're comfortable with Claude reading.
TranscriptA written record of a meeting or call, usually from a recording.
PromptThe instruction you give Claude.
Scheduled taskA saved prompt that Cowork runs on a schedule (hourly, daily, weekly, weekdays) or on demand.
ApprovalCowork can ask your permission before taking an action. A scheduled task has an approval setting too.
Synthetic dataMade-up data that looks real. Everything in our kit is synthetic.
