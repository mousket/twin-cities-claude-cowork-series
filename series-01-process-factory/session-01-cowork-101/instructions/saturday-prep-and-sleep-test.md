# Prep and the "does it run while the lid is closed?" test

Public documentation about scheduled tasks and sleeping computers has been inconsistent, so test on your demo laptop, with the exact setup you will use Tuesday.

## Saturday, October 3
1. Unzip the kit to a clean folder on the demo laptop.
2. Open Cowork, grant access to the kit's `birchwood-folder`, create the task **Monday Action Digest** with the kit prompt, schedule it **daily** at a time soon after your usual wake-up. (Daily on purpose: you want several runs of history by Tuesday.)
3. Run it once with Run now so you see a file in `digests/`.
4. Leave the Claude app open but let the laptop sleep overnight with the lid closed. Note the scheduled time and the exact state: plugged in or not, lid closed, app open or not.

## Sunday, October 4
- Check whether Saturday night's and Sunday morning's runs happened, and when. Write down exactly what you saw, with timestamps.
- Dry run the whole session on a stopwatch. Record it on your phone.

## Monday, October 5
- Check Sunday and Monday runs. Decide what you will say on Tuesday: "runs when…" in your own words, based on what you observed.
- Update the sample digest if the real output has a better structure.

## Tuesday, October 6
- Arrive 5:00. Do one Run now so there is a fresh digest.
- Keep the laptop plugged in and awake for Part 3.

## What to record in the gotchas log
Date, what you did, what happened, what you'd tell an attendee. Keep it in `instructions/gotchas-log.md` (create it Saturday).
