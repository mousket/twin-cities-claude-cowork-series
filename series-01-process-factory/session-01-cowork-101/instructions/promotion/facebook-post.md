# Facebook post (for your network; post today, Monday Oct 5)

Tomorrow night I'm leading a free workshop in Minneapolis, and I built it for regular people who are curious about AI but don't know where to start.

No jargon, no coding. We'll pretend to be the office manager of a small company, the kind with a messy shared folder, meetings where nobody writes down who's doing what, and a Monday to-do list that lives in someone's head. Then we'll see how an AI helper can read the pile, pull out the action items, and write the weekly follow-up for us. We'll also practice the most important skill: checking its work, because it does get things wrong.

The point isn't to be impressed by AI. It's to get some time and peace of mind back for the things that matter more than paperwork.

It's free. Bring a laptop if you have one, but you don't need one. One honest heads-up: the tool we use needs a paid Claude plan to try hands-on. If you don't have one, come sit with a friend and watch. You'll still get everything we make, including a step-by-step guide you can follow at home.

Tuesday, Oct 6, 6:00 to 7:30 PM, Twin Ignition Startup Garage in Minneapolis. Free parking, coffee and snacks.

Details and sign-up: https://www.meetup.com/twin-cities-claude-agentic-ai-meetup/events/316670014/

If you know someone who keeps saying "I should figure out AI," send them this. Bring them along!
