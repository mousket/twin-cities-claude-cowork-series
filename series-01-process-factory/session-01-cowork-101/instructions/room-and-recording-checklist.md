## Recording setup (simple, reliable)
1. **Screen:** record the presenter laptop screen with its built-in screen recorder or OBS. Record locally first; streaming is optional.
2. **Audio:** a wireless lapel mic into the laptop or a recorder, plus a backup phone recording of the room audio.
3. **Camera:** optional; point at the presenter and screen only, not the audience.
4. **Q&A:** repeat every question into the mic before answering. This is the single biggest quality upgrade.
5. **Do Not Disturb on.** Close email, messaging and personal tabs. Use a clean profile so your account info, notifications and other tasks don't appear.
6. **Consent:** a sign at the door and a spoken notice at 6:00. Attendees can sit out of frame. Don't show attendee screens.
7. **After:** upload to a private link, trim the front, add chapter marks (Part 1, 2, 3, Wrap), generate captions, then publish unlisted. Check that no real data or secrets appear on screen.
