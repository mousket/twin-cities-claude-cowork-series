# At home: do Session 1 on your own

You can do the whole session without the presenter. Start here:

1. **Check what you need.** `../pre-session/01-before-you-start.html` (or the published page listed in `PUBLISHED.md` at the repo root). About 10 minutes.
2. **Get the kit.** `../pre-session/02-get-the-starter-kit.html`. About 5 minutes.
3. **Follow the plan.** `../starter-kit/SESSION-PLAN.md`. It also sits in the kit you download, so you can open it in VS Code beside the folder. Allow 45 to 90 minutes.
4. **Check your understanding.** `../instructions/concepts-and-terminology/` has the glossary and concepts.

The prompts for each part are in `../starter-kit/prompts/`.
